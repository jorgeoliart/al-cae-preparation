⚠ Active traps in this set
Q8 Wrong element extracted: the audio says "top five exhibitions of natural science" — the frame's superlative idea is already encoded. Write natural science, not top five.
Q9 Instruction blindness — double gap: the frame shows two blanks. Both butterflies and birds are required. One word alone does not receive the mark.
Q12 Wrong noun phrase extracted: "million-pound refit" is prominent in the audio but answers a different question. The frame — "the museum's ___ needs modernising" — requires central heating.
