Comparison Structures

Open cloze — comparative frames, degree adverbs & fixed expressions  ·  Phrasal verbs: comparison & evaluation

Grammar Reference
StructureExampleNote
as + adj/adv + as The results were as clear as expected. equality comparison
not as / not so + adj + as The margin was not as wide as predicted. not so is more formal
the + comparative…the + comparative The more data collected, the clearer the picture. both clauses need the
no sooner…than No sooner had she spoken than the alarm sounded. inversion required after no sooner
hardly / scarcely…when / before Hardly had the meeting ended when objections arose. inversion required; when or before
no + comparative + than The new method is no more reliable than the old one. no negates the comparative
⚡ The…the structures require the definite article before both comparatives — missing the second the is the most common error. The subject–verb order in each clause is statement order, not inverted.
AdverbUseExample
far / by far large degree; formal far more effective  /  by far the best
much large degree; neutral much higher than expected

a great deal large degree; semi-formal a great deal faster
rather moderate; British formal hedging rather more cautious
slightly / a little small degree slightly higher  /  a little faster
⚡ Position rule: all degree adverbs precede the comparative — far more reliable, never more far reliable. By far is the exception — it precedes a superlative: by far the most significant.
compared with / toboth correct; with more formalCompared with last year, output rose.
in comparison withformal; interchangeable with compared withIn comparison with rivals, costs are low.
by contraststandalone discourse markerBy contrast, the second group improved.
in contrast tofollowed immediately by noun/gerundIn contrast to earlier findings…

as opposed toemphasises contrast of two alternativesQuality, as opposed to quantity, matters.

stand in contrast toformal; subject is the contrasted itemThis stands in stark contrast to…
⚡ By contrast vs in contrast to: by contrast is a standalone sentence adverbial followed by a comma. In contrast to must be followed directly by a noun or gerund phrase — never a full clause.
ConnectorFollowed byExample
although / even though / though full clause Although costs rose, output improved.
despite / in spite of noun / gerund (-ing) Despite rising costs, output improved.
whereas / while full clause (contrast) Whereas Group A improved, Group B declined.
however / nevertheless / nonetheless sentence adverbial + comma The data was limited. However, the trends were clear.
yet coordinating conjunction or adverbial Costs rose, yet output improved.
⚡ Despite / in spite of cannot be followed by a full clause. The most common Part 2 error is writing despite + clause instead of despite + -ing or despite the fact that + clause.

Comparison & Evaluation

Semantic Field
measure up (to)

stand out (from)
to be noticeably better or different from others in a group
weigh up
to carefully consider the pros and cons of two or more options
set apart (from)
to make someone or something clearly different or superior
come close (to)
to almost match or equal something; to nearly achieve a standard
size up
to carefully assess or evaluate someone or something
